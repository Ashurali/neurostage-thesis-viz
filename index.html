System Architecture

End-to-end pipeline from raw EEG to interpretable classification.

Three Feature Domains

Each domain captures a distinct dimension of brain activity.

PSD

"What frequencies dominate?"

Power Spectral Density measures how much signal energy exists at each frequency band across all channels.

19 channels × 6 bands = 114 features

Coherence

"Are brain regions in sync?"

Coherence quantifies how synchronized the oscillations are between every pair of electrode channels at different frequencies.

171 channel pairs × 4 bands = 684 features

Microstate

"How does the brain switch states?"

Microstates are stable, recurring topographic configurations of scalp electrical potential — the "atoms" of thought that last 60–120 ms.

58 temporal + 76 spatial = 134 features

ReliefF selects features that differ most between cognitive groups while staying consistent within each group — dropping irrelevant or noisy dimensions. This reduces the 932-dimensional feature space to just 30 highly informative features without losing discriminative power.

Two-Stage Ensemble Classifier

Hierarchical classification mirrors how clinicians reason.

Fusion Mechanism: Out-of-Fold Stacking

  • ● Base learners: 3 independent Random Forest classifiers (one per feature domain), no parameter sharing
  • ● Meta-learner: Logistic regression trained on out-of-fold (OOF) probabilities, minimizing cross-entropy loss
  • ● Inference: Weights frozen after training — no per-sample re-tuning, ensuring reproducibility
\[P_{\text{final}}(c \mid x) = \sigma\!\left(w_0 + w_{\text{PSD}}\,\hat{p}_{\text{PSD}}(c \mid x) + w_{\text{Coh}}\,\hat{p}_{\text{Coh}}(c \mid x) + w_{\text{Mic}}\,\hat{p}_{\text{Mic}}(c \mid x)\right)\]

σ = sigmoid · p̂_d(c|x) = OOF predicted probability from domain d · w_d = fixed fusion weights

NeuroStage is positioned for clinical translation rather than peak benchmark performance. Compared to Sun et al. (2024) — the nearest multi-domain comparator — NeuroStage uses fixed-weight fusion and full LOSO validation across every subject, prioritizing reproducibility and stage-wise interpretability over single-fold accuracy maxima. The 87.9% MCI-stage accuracy is competitive with prior MCI-specific work while remaining auditable for clinical workflows.

Future Directions

Building on this foundation toward clinical deployment.

01

Multi-center Validation

Extend the cohort across hospitals and equipment under unified IRB to assess cross-site generalization beyond the current single-center design.

02

Variance & Confidence Reporting

Report bootstrap confidence intervals on LOSO accuracy and per-fold variance to formally quantify the stability claimed in the model name.

03

Modern Feature Selection Benchmarks

Compare ReliefF against contemporary methods (Boruta, mRMR, SHAP-guided iterative selection) to validate whether the top-10-per-domain configuration is empirically optimal.

04

Deep Learning Baselines

Benchmark NeuroStage against EEGNet, ShallowConvNet, and Transformer-based EEG models on the same data and same LOSO protocol to formally characterize the interpretability-versus-accuracy trade-off.

05

Statistical Testing of Microstate D

Apply cluster-based permutation testing on microstate D's spatial topology to formally validate the NC-versus-MCI deformation observation reported here.

06

Closed-loop Clinical Integration

Couple NeuroStage with real-time EEG streaming and adaptive non-invasive stimulation (e.g., tDCS, TMS) toward the closed-loop diagnosis-and-therapy system motivated in the introduction.
